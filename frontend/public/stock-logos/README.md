Company logo images retrieved on 8 November 2026 from public stock identification assets at https://assets-netstorage.groww.in/stock-assets/logos2/{NSE_SYMBOL}.webp.

Trademarks and logos belong to their respective companies. Their display identifies securities and does not imply endorsement or affiliation with PaperTradeX or Groww. Assets are served locally; no visitor data is sent to that image host. See src/lib/stock-logos.ts for the available symbol manifest. Unavailable symbols use a neutral initials fallback.

Symbols outside the local manifest are looked up through the same-origin /api/company-logo endpoint, using https://images.dhan.co/symbol/{SYMBOL}.png and the Groww asset host as a fallback. Only the public ticker is sent by the server. Successful and unavailable results are cached, and unresolved images fall back to initials.
